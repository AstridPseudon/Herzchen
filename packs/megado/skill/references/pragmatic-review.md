# Proportionate verification and review

The manager selects the cheapest sufficient evidence for the outcome. Consider downside, blast radius, reversibility, what can be tested or tried directly and the residual question. Record a short rationale at a material boundary, not a risk score or form per edit.

| Need | Primary method | Independent model-review allowance when selected |
|---|---|---|
| Reversible and directly verifiable work | Relevant tests, public-tool use and manager inspection | 0; no implicit final reviewer |
| Bounded local judgment where an independent reading adds information | `reviewer_normal`: Luna Extra High, separate leaf | Normally one selected response |
| Irreducible nonlocal reasoning with meaningful downside and limited observability | Failure/recovery experiments plus `reviewer_xhard`: Sol High | One initial; at most a targeted second when adopted |
| Consequential direction or a holistic integrated result | Real end-to-end evidence plus configured Astra High oracle/final role | Normally one; at most two for an explicitly selected holistic review stage |

These choices are alternatives, not a Luna-then-Sol-then-Astra pipeline. A manager using Luna Extra High can inspect ordinary work in its existing turn; that is not an independent review. A selected reviewer is a fresh leaf even when it uses the same model as the manager or worker. Worker route never automatically determines reviewer route. A high reasoning setting does not imply XHARD classification or new authority.

The seven role slots remain configurable. This edition's normal reviewer is Luna Extra High and XHARD reviewer Sol High. An adopted instance may explicitly select Sol for a particular important technical gate. Never silently downgrade that gate after a default change, or invoke the normal reviewer before it for reassurance. Actual runner support is verified rather than invented.

Templates grant no extra scope or budget. Select only distinct boundaries where their information can change a decision. Prefer tests/use when those settle the question. Preserve current mandatory reviews, independent advisors, identities and actual consumption. Changing route, model, title or session cannot refresh a scope's allowance; a new additional review needs a genuine selected scope and existing explicit authority. A final model review is not mandatory for directly testable ordinary work.

Before review, run the relevant checks and assemble their existing evidence. Do not ask reviewers to discover known incomplete work, rerun a whole suite by habit or meet a quota of findings. First reviews see the applicable contract and selected question; repeat reviews focus on changed findings and affected dependencies.

Keep findings as contract_violation, implementation_defect, required_evidence_gap, optional_improvement, out_of_scope or stale_or_repeated. Only the first three can block when evidenced and within scope. Correction difficulty is separate from blocking severity. The manager chooses useful correction, investigation, waiting or a designated decision; software never runs a feedback loop from prose alone.

An original reviewer result can supply an objective closure rule: named check, expected outcome, bounded correction, and exact candidate/criteria basis. An independent factual executor produces proof and the accountable owner may close the finding under that rule without another model opinion. Preserve the original REWORK result. Subjective findings, broader changes, contradictions or UNKNOWN/missing results cannot be converted into mechanical closure after the fact. At a cap preserve the unresolved requirement and seek only the missing decision/allowance; do not invent PASS or discard useful authorised work.

Unknown/possibly invoked responses remain held or charged. Packet assembly and ordinary test execution are not certification calls. Do not relabel a review as debugging, advice or optimisation to evade limits. Preserve actual costs and explicit purpose, including scheduled checks where adopted. The goal is reliable useful progress with minimum necessary coordination, not maximal review activity.
